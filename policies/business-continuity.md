# VE×A Public Continuity and Recovery Policy

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

## Objective

VE×A plans for the secure restoration of essential services and authorized data after technology, provider, security, or operational disruption. Recovery must not bypass access control, evidence integrity, quarantine, retention, legal hold, or tenant-isolation requirements.

## Recovery principles

- prioritize safety, evidence integrity, and critical investigative functions;
- define recovery scope and authorization before enrolling production data;
- keep recovery copies protected and logically separated from active processing;
- validate restored information before it becomes available for ordinary use;
- measure recovery outcomes and retain exercise evidence privately;
- account for provider dependencies and manual operating alternatives; and
- use lessons from exercises and incidents to improve plans and controls.

Initial acceptance exercises use controlled synthetic data. Expansion to ordinary or restricted production data requires explicit scope approval and the appropriate retention, legal-hold, encryption, isolation, and restore controls.
