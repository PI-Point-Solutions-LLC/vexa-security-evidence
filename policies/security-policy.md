# VE×A Public Security Policy

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

## Purpose and scope

PI Point Solutions, LLC maintains a risk-based security program for VE×A across product development, production operations, authorized investigative data, personnel access, service providers, and continuity activities. This public policy states security outcomes; detailed standards, architecture, procedures, and evidence remain restricted.

## Security objectives

VE×A will:

- authorize access according to platform, organization, case, and evidence-handling responsibilities;
- isolate customer and case information and protect it in transit, at rest, during processing, and during recovery;
- preserve evidence provenance, integrity, uncertainty, and review history;
- treat untrusted content as unsafe until required controls complete;
- restrict privileged operations and sensitive evidence handling to explicitly authorized personnel and services;
- record security-relevant activity and maintain monitoring appropriate to risk;
- review software, dependencies, infrastructure, and providers throughout their lifecycle;
- respond to vulnerabilities and incidents with containment, evidence preservation, communication, remediation, and improvement;
- test continuity and recovery controls using controlled data before expanding production scope; and
- maintain separate sanitized public assurance evidence and detailed private audit evidence.

## Governance

The VE×A owner is accountable for security risk acceptance and policy approval. Material changes require security review and retained evidence. Exceptions require a documented scope, rationale, owner, expiration or review date, and compensating controls.

## Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after a material security incident, architecture change, regulatory change, or investigative-data-flow change.
