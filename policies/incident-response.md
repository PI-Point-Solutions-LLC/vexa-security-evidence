# VE×A Public Incident Response Policy

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

## Objective

VE×A maintains an incident-response capability to identify, contain, investigate, remediate, recover from, and learn from events that threaten confidentiality, integrity, availability, lawful use, or evidence trust.

## Lifecycle

The response lifecycle covers preparation, detection and analysis, containment, preservation of relevant records, eradication, recovery, required notification, and post-incident improvement. Response decisions consider customer and case isolation, evidence integrity, legal and contractual duties, service-provider coordination, and the risk of disrupting active investigations.

## Authority and communication

The VE×A owner is accountable for incident declaration, material risk decisions, and external communications. Access to detailed incident records is restricted. Customers and affected parties are notified when required and appropriate based on validated scope, legal obligations, investigative sensitivity, and available facts.

## Evidence and exercises

Incidents and exercises retain dated decisions, actions, validation results, exceptions, and remediation ownership in the private evidence record. Public reporting is limited to safe, authorized summaries.

Status: **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** — containment and audit capabilities operate; broader tabletop, notification, and service-failure exercises remain in progress.
