# Security Control Ownership

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

VE×A currently uses consolidated ownership appropriate to a small organization. Role consolidation does not remove review, evidence, or separation-of-duty requirements where the same person performs multiple functions.

| Control function | Accountable owner | Minimum review cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security program and risk acceptance | VE×A owner | Quarterly and after material change |
| Secure development and release approval | VE×A owner | Every production release |
| Privacy, lawful authority, and data minimization | VE×A owner | Quarterly and after data-flow change |
| Identity, privileged access, and service credentials | VE×A owner | Quarterly and after role change |
| Evidence integrity and case isolation | VE×A owner | On change and quarterly |
| Vendor and supply-chain risk | VE×A owner | Risk-ranked cadence and after material change |
| Incident response and communications | VE×A owner | After incident or exercise; contacts annually |
| Continuity, backup, and recovery | VE×A owner | Quarterly evidence review; exercise at least annually |
| Public security evidence and disclosure review | VE×A owner | Each material release and quarterly |

Independent assessment is not currently claimed. Restricted records identify the actual reviewer and retained evidence.
